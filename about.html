About Vivly Living

Building homes with care, quality, and sustainability in the heart of West Yorkshire since 2009.

Our Story

A heritage of quality

Formerly known as Yorkshire Country Properties, Vivly Living was born from a passion for creating homes that truly make a difference to people's lives. Based in Shepley, we're a team of local experts who know and love the rolling hills of West Yorkshire.

Our rebrand to Vivly Living reflects our evolution — but our core values remain unchanged. We still build homes with the same dedication to superior design, craftsmanship, and respect for the communities we build in.

What Drives Us

Our values

Three principles guide everything we do — from selecting our sites to welcoming you into your new home.

⭐

Quality

Every Vivly home features premium specifications as standard — from SieMatic designer kitchens to luxury bathroom suites. We don't cut corners; we create homes built to last.

🌱

Sustainability

Air source heat pumps, solar panels, EV charging and A-rated energy efficiency come as standard. We build homes that help you live comfortably, consciously, and cost-effectively.

🤝

Community

We only build in carefully chosen village locations where our homes complement the existing community. We use characterful materials that respect the heritage of each area.

Personalisation

As individual as you

We believe your home should reflect who you are. That's why every Vivly home comes with a range of customisation options — from kitchen cabinets and worktops to bathroom tiles, flooring, and wardrobes.

Visit our Customer Experience Hub to explore finishes and fittings hands-on, or use our online kitchen visualiser to design your dream kitchen from home.
